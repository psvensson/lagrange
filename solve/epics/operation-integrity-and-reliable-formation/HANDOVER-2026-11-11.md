## What was repaired (unit / real-group level; see the log for detail and proofs)

1. **Operation workflow** — durable-first steps; committed-voter credit for remove
   safety; three-state by-id read contract (PRESENT / ABSENT / UNAVAILABLE) and
   three-state list contract, with ~35 consumers reclassified so a failed read never
   discharges, drops, duplicates or admits; REPLACE STOPPING write is an intent, never
   an owed write.
2. **Leader removal** — coordinator gate for REPLACE and plain REMOVE on every
   partition class; node side: a replica never proposes its own RemoveNode, hands
   leadership to a survivor, the leader's port refuses removing itself, a removed
   leader steps down; the leader refuses a removal that leaves no live quorum;
   `CANCEL_REPLICA_REMOVAL` when a REPLACE retains its source; a spent wait never
   retires a voter; participant custody read from the file before either irreversible
   step.
3. **Planner, fence, provisioning** — candidate readiness is READY / NOT_READY /
   DEFERRED with a publication wake; transient admission denials wait on the event
   that clears them (publication, route-table change, the routing owner's retry-due
   instant); partial founding is completed by the schema job's retry and released if
   the job ends; the release fence holds on founding work and priority transitions,
   never on planner moves, and needs a cluster-wide owner census to read CLEAR; serial
   gate, remove-safety concurrency and the global in-flight count hold on unanswered
   reads.
4. **Restart recovery** — `handleRecovery` had no production caller and is deleted with
   its fail-on-restart decisions; a restart never fails an operation whose effect may
   be in flight.
5. **Transport** — ACK vs answer contract with three timeout reasons
   (`ack_timeout`, `answer_timeout`, `unestablished_timeout`); socket-specific
   quarantine evidence; keepalive derived from the liveness window (10 s); exhaustive
   error-code classification table.
6. **CDC** — range shipping; per-recipient cursors with named waits and honoured
   backoff; one propagation subscriber per replica; occurrence identity by event key;
   Stage 3 start position (ledger incarnation, source ranges, ALREADY_APPLIED / GAP,

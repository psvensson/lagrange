## What each consumer should call after these changes

(From the source record sections 14-16 and the lead's note of 2026-11-21.)

- **`currentTermApplied`** now means exactly "this replica leads and the term of the last entry it applied is its
  current term" (an entry of its own term applied; monotone within a term): leader and `appliedTerm === term`, from
  the runtime owner's `appliedTerm` (set on every applied slice, on an installed snapshot, restored at opening). It
  no longer reads false after the leader applies its own configuration change, and it is false after a restart
  until the new term's entry applies. The recorded status observation carries the `appliedTerm` it was read with.
  `confChangePending` is unchanged (it means "a proposed configuration change not yet applied").
- **The current core observation:** `port.observeCurrentAuthority()` (`RAFT_OPERATION.OBSERVE_CURRENT_AUTHORITY`):
  not a command, never a turn, never queued; two core reads (`status`, `has_ready`) and the owner's own fields; no
  step, drain, Ready, persistence, application, announcement or recorded observation. Answer (CORE_OK):
  `groupId, replicaIdentity, peerId, settled (= !turnPending && !readyHeld), turnPending, readyHeld, inboundQueued,
  role, term, leaderPeerId, commitIndex, appliedIndex, appliedTerm, currentTermApplied, lifecycleIncarnation,
  runtimeGeneration, runtimeHealth, groupHealth`. `role` and `leaderPeerId` are
  `RAFT_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION.NOT_SETTLED` for a core that leads, has not applied an entry of its term and is not
  settled. A runtime or group that cannot be read answers its hold as it stands. `readStatus()` is unchanged (the
  last completed entry's status while a turn is pending, and it drives delivered inbound).
- **Placement owner** (`replica-placement-owner-tenure.js` `isTermStartApplied`): read `currentTermApplied` and
  `term` from `port.observeCurrentAuthority()`, not `readStatus()`; its comment should say "applied an entry of its
  own term".
- **Owner-fact receiver** (`partition-service-remote-query-methods.js` `answerLeaderQualifiedRead`, both the decision
  and the post-read re-check in `leaderReadRefusalAfter`): call `observeCurrentAuthority()` (through the service's
  own seam) for the decision and rely on: `outcome === CORE_OK` (anything else refuses); `role === LEADER`
  (`NOT_SETTLED` and every other role refuse); `currentTermApplied === true` (its `electionLogApplied` witness field
  should be this, not `confChangePending === false`); `term`; `appliedIndex` (the rows' position);
  `lifecycleIncarnation`; `runtimeGeneration`. Not `commitIndex` as a position (in memory, may be ahead of applied).
  The redirect (`resolveLeaderAddress`) may stay on `readStatus()`.
- **Proposers** (item C): a proposal enters the core only under the leadership it was submitted under (the core's
  term as last announced, recorded at submission). A core that does not lead, or leads another term, never
  proposes or forwards: the proposal answers the core's own drop, `CORE_REFUSED`, reason
  `propose: raft: proposal dropped`, phase `propose`, `retryable: false`. Follower forwarding of MsgPropose is gone;
  no production caller relied on it (the write kernel types the refusal "not proposed", the message group a routing
  miss).

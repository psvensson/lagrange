### 4.3 Can the terminal record carry the SERVICES commit position of its effect? [V]
- The SERVICES row of an ADD is written on the TARGET node by its handler (`registerReplica` then
  activation). The named write's answer carries `durableCommitWitness` {partitionId, term, logIndex,
  entryId} when the real engine answers (`partition-write-kernel.js:307-322`; carried through the producers
  since 6144769da), but `registerReplica` returns only the sealed row (`partition-service-row-owner.js:330-337`)
  and the CREATE answer does not forward it: today the position never reaches the coordinator.
- The coordinator completes the operation on its own observation of the row,
  `getActualReplicaObservation` (`replica-operation-repository-observation-methods.js:487-539`). That read's
  answer carries `readAuthorityWitness` {term, appliedIndex} when a leader serves it, and the observation
  drops it (:530-538). It is OWNER_RPC_PREFERRED with leader ANY, so it may be a follower's answer.
- Cheapest carrier: make the completion read leader-qualified and record its witness's SERVICES
  {partitionId, term, appliedIndex} on the terminal transition (steps-history metadata); appliedIndex >= the
  row's commit index, so as a floor it is safe (errs toward refusal). Alternative: the target forwards its
  registration write's `durableCommitWitness` in the CREATE answer (exact position; touches the handler and
  dispatch-response reconcile). Neither exists today. REMOVE: the source row's terminal status is written by
  the source node's removal path and later cleaned up by the tombstone owner; the later deletion is not on
  the operation's terminal record [I].

## Section A: coherence of P

Mechanism: not a SQLite transaction on the reader's side but single-threaded synchronous execution on one connection. The partition passes `this.db` as the port request's DURABLE_STORAGE (`partition-service-raft-init-base.js:452`), the port reads it as `database` (`raft-rs-operation-port.js:162-163`) and the runtime constructs `new RaftRsDurableStore(request.database)` (`raft-rs-runtime-owner.js:1893`); GROUP_ID is `this.partitionId` (`:439`). The apply writes the data rows, the ledger rows and `putAppliedState` in one `store.transaction` (`raft-rs-application-transaction-owner.js:60-84`). `readHydrationImage` runs `statement.all()` and `.get()` back to back with no `await` (`partition-service-hydration-image-methods.js:43-51`); no `worker_threads` under `src/raft` or `src/partition`, so no apply callback can interleave. Therefore the image holds exactly the entries at or below P. Confirmed live: P equals `readDurableProgress` at the same instant in W-D4a (both K) and in my probe; the design's probe fact "applied 24 vs ledger max 22" shows why MAX(log_index) would be wrong (CDC_DELIVERED entries advance the applied index without a ledger row).

Leader change: see F7. A leadership change after registration resets the lane (`resetCdcObligationLane`, `partition-cdc-obligation-delivery.js:147-157`) so pending events become owed again on the new leader; the registered subscribers on this node remain registered on all three local replicas.

## Section B: the reorder

Per table (`hydrateTableFromLocalPartition`, `:403-452`): resolve `INITIAL_PARTITION_IDS[table]` → `registerSubscribers(table)` = `subscribeToInitialSystemTableCDC(table)` → `subscribeToHydrationCDC` registers message-group handlers then `await partition.subscribeToCDCWithHandshake(...)` for every replica × every message group → find the local leader → `readHydrationImage(SELECT * FROM table)` → apply each row with `applyCDCEvent(..., skipReplication, skipSubscriptionCheck)` → `TABLE_HYDRATED` with `appliedIndex` → `result.tables[table] = {success, rowCount, appliedIndex}`. The handshake fires a non-awaited `driveCdcObligations(SUBSCRIPTION_HANDSHAKE)`; owed history is then delivered to the propagation subscribers, which refuse until `ensureLatencyTopologyOwners` (F2) and are retried, bounded by `CDC_BUFFER_REPLAY_MAX_DELAY_MS` = 10 s. The leader wait follows every table (phase test "completes every table's handoff before the leader wait" green) and needs no CDC (probe 4).

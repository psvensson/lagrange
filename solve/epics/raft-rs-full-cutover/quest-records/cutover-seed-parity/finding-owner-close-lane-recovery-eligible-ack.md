# Finding: the owner's close lane acknowledges for a recovery-eligible candidate member (RECOVERY_ELIGIBLE_ACK)

Recorded 2026-09-24 in the round-3 repair of cutover seed parity. Not changed in this unit (owner decision).

- **Where:** `src/control-plane/membership-publication-target-selection.js:67-83`, rule `MEMBERSHIP_PUBLICATION_ACK_CARRY_STATE.RECOVERY_ELIGIBLE_ACK` in `MEMBERSHIP_PUBLICATION_ACK_CARRY_RULES`, applied by `resolveMembershipPublicationAcknowledgedNodeIds`. When a reconcile re-derives an unchanged candidate (`publicationChanged !== true`), the rule carries every `publishableRecoveryActiveNodeIds` member into `acknowledgedNodeIds`. It does this whether or not that node acknowledged the candidate. This is CL-001 variant A, pre-existing.
- **What round 3 changed around it:** the active-gate handoff target no longer acknowledges for a pending candidate member that has not acknowledged the candidate (`withholdUnacknowledgedCandidateNodeIds` in `publication-active-gate-handoff-contract-selection.js`). The owner's drivers now carry that candidate under its own name (`buildPublicationActiveGateMembershipConvergence`). Witnesses: `membership-publication-first-epoch-members.test.js`, the two "never acknowledges for a pending candidate's unacknowledged member" tests, which check the first driven reconcile.
- **Observed:** probe `hb3.mjs` in the implementer scratchpad. Epoch 1 is PUBLISHED [seed]; the OPEN candidate [joiner, seed] has ack [seed]; a third READY node is missing. Tick 1 writes ACK_PENDING [joiner, seed, third] with ack [seed, third], so the joiner stays unacknowledged. On ticks 2-3 the owner re-drives the unchanged candidate, and RECOVERY_ELIGIBLE_ACK carries the READY, recovery-eligible joiner into `acknowledgedNodeIds`. The row becomes PUBLISHED [joiner, seed, third] without the joiner ever acknowledging.
- **Why it matters:** "candidate members stay unacknowledged until they acknowledge" holds for the handoff target, but not for the owner's close lane. That lane substitutes recovery eligibility (a readiness observation) for the member's own acknowledgement. The same lane is what closes OPEN publications whose members cannot reach the acknowledgement path (CL-001 variant A). Removing it without a replacement acknowledgement route would reintroduce the stuck-OPEN epoch.
- **Owner decision:** either keep the close lane as the defined substitute for acknowledgement and state it in the membership publication contract, or require an actual acknowledgement and route the acknowledgement for recovery-eligible members through `acknowledgeMembershipPublicationForNode`. The second option needs a witness for the stuck-OPEN case first.

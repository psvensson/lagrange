The old census also contained the address-authority test (Group B) with an *inert peer-progress probe* cause, not today's handler-identity mock cause, and the write-pacing test (Group D) already red on `main`.

**2026-09-18: separate same-family five-node cold-formation failure.** `solve/epics/formation-seed-decoupling/causal-packet-2026-09-18.md` compares three same-source nights (Sep 15 PASS, Sep 16 FAIL, Sep 17 PASS). It identifies a planner-authorized over-target spread ADD not carrying its authorization to the learner's promotion guard. The two owners re-derived the same permission differently, leaving learners blocked until their 60-second promotion deadline. The prescribed root fix is **one captured, durable authorizing decision delivered to its downstream consumer**, never local grant guessing or increasing the timeout. **2026-07-04 to 2026-07-19: write pacing and participant movement.** `solve/quests/write-path-internal-pacing/log.ndjson` records control-plane's *own* operation-row write failing with `DISTRIBUTED_PARTICIPANT_FAILURE` during REPLACE (Jul 4), later a well-formed caller timed out during split under saturated event loop (Jul 19), and deterministic evidence of a partition mutating local SQLite *before* Raft commit, with demotion failing to release the original write. The 2026-10-20 `write-path-internal-pacing` initial failures differ between predecessor (cutover pause) and candidate (no leader); diagnose before reusing any old patch.

**2026-09-30: exact replica transport handler retirement.** Commit [`1aea11baf`](https://github.com/psvensson/lagrange/commit/1aea11baf6ba35a397547be54fa2621e674c1977) introduced the exact-handler retirement owner and associated tests. **Locality:** **proved local to this test double's missing method** on the exact measured source. **Remember this as L-01**. The *pattern* (test double silently drifting from production interface) is systemic and recurred in U1 REPLACE; future test doubles should be contract-checked, not patched through production fallbacks.

# Replica Placement Admission Owner — Stage 3b R09 Supersession & Proof-Transfer Record

## 1. Context & Scope

In Stage 3a/3b of `replica-placement-admission-owner`, the per-partition `UnifiedRebalancer` construction path in `PartitionService` and the `this.placementAdmissionEnabled === true` guard (`R2`) in `RebalanceCoordinator.createOperationInternal` (`src/rebalancer/rebalance-coordinator-operation-creation.js`) are removed.

On every production node (`ControlPlaneSetup.create`), `bindReplicaPlacementOwnerFactory` is called synchronously during coordinator construction, and partition / runtime-service operations take `createOperationViaPlacementAdmission` -> `requestPlacementAdmission` -> `applyPlacementCommand` -> `decidePlacementAdmission` on the `replica_operations-p1` Raft leader. The record creation path (`createOperationRecordInternal`, `createOperationWithinConcurrentCreateBudgetTurn`, and creation-time `INSERT OR IGNORE INTO storage_reservations`) remains the production creation path for non-managed entity types (`message_group`), while `OperationWorkflowOwner` continues to own post-creation dispatch, remove safety, learner promotion, timeout reconciliation, and `ensureReservationForOperation` at the dispatch gate.

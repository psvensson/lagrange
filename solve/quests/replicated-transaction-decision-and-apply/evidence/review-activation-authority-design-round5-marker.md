## Complete findings

### R5-1 — HIGH — a superseded partial share can authorize before the final decision

D:1725 calls each READY share an **irrevocable vote**, and D:1750–1757 explicitly retains it if that member changes after voting. The only authorization decision is later, when the final share applies (D:1739–1745). These are different boundaries.

Concrete schedule: reserve control configuration K; member A records a share under lifecycle/connection/build A1; delay B's final share; A restarts or changes its connection/build to A2 without a membership change; record B's current share. The slot still has K and A1's recorded share, so final application grants RESUME_AUTHORIZED. The membership gate never excluded A's lifecycle change (D:1714–1716). D:1733 also makes a replacement share for the same member conflicting rather than describing a current-owner supersession transition. Thus evidence known only to have been current at an earlier, partial vote becomes authority for a later release decision.

This contradicts the requested current-incarnation requirement **before final authorization**. It is not a demand that a fully committed decision be revoked by a later restart, or that a process remain alive until message delivery. Actual owner facts include owner/lifecycle/runtime/connection/admission generations (`src/partition/partition-participant-release-facts.js:72–78,89–116`); the current local admission owner invalidates stale runtime/lifecycle/term/leader/config proofs (`src/partition/partition-participant-transaction-pause.js:41–51,63–79`). A membership-only slot cannot certify these facts by itself.

**Required correction:** specify how the current incarnation authority fences or supersedes an already-recorded partial share before the last-share decision, with an explicit pending/refused result and same-owner reentry. Preserve durable effect recovery after a genuinely complete committed authorization. Do not silently redefine the user’s current-census contract as irrevocable partial votes. The required latch is **after A's share is committed and before B's final share**, with A superseded in between. D:1768–1773 currently tests a missing old share and then conflates “after its share” with “already authorized”; that misses this schedule.

### R5-2 — HIGH — zero-slot recovery lacks its own resource bound and canonical payload byte bound

The pre-control phase is the right place to break the old cycle. However D:1572–1606 exempts it from all four ordinary cohort slots and lets it revalidate/obtain fresh authorization, query dependent state, commit PAUSE, and continue REMOVE. Its only stated coalescing key is `[controlPartitionId,reservationId]`. D:864–880 and :1184–1216 quantify ordinary phases, pending entries and fairness; they do not bound this new phase's simultaneous operations, retired-key waiters, reply buffers, retry/log cadence or coexistence with four saturated ordinary phases. A single durable slot limits winning authority, not outstanding JS/RPC work from successive reservations, leader/service changes or late replies.

The new wire/durable sets likewise have a membership count bound (D:1741–1745,1792) but no named maximum encoded bytes or referenced byte-limit owner. Strict primitive strings and at most 1024 elements do not bound materialized share/manifest/receipt bytes. Whole canonical sets are accumulated, sorted, hashed and persisted; the before-materialization count-and-byte check requested for this boundary is unspecified. This is a release-message bound, not a new transaction-participant cap.

The actual scheduler has one four-entry running gate and one coalesced partition map (`src/node/replica-handler-release-recovery.js:17–23,40–55,97–118`), plus repeated-signature logging suppression (:58–67). No separate preflight accounting can be inferred from those existing mechanisms.

**Required correction:** in that same owner, define the preflight's numeric concurrent-work limit, canonical running key versus current reservation revision, retained pending/late-reply limits, retirement/shutdown behavior and retry/log cadence. Charge its member/RPC work and bytes while all ordinary slots are occupied; distinguish a bounded recovery execution attempt from the lifetime of its committed debt. Specify or reference the canonical encoded-byte limits and enforce count plus bytes before fanout/materialization/hash/row allocation. Keep priority without a second scheduler or an uncounted capacity bypass. Required witness saturates four slots, changes the control leader/reservation while a preflight reply is withheld, and observes bounded retained work plus eventual canonical reentry.

### R5-3 — HIGH — follower OPENED receipts precede a transfer that invalidates them

D:1797–1812 opens and confirms followers, then performs the “existing leader-transfer step,” then authorizes the former leader and clears the slot using the accumulated local receipt set. The actual existing transfer is part of **connection reopening before the final census/resume**, and explicitly returns STALE so a new attempt observes the term/cohort (`src/node/replica-handler-participant-cutover.js:171–197`; final census/resume :278–299). It is not an interposed step after followers have resumed.

Actual admission closes on term or leader change, independently of lifecycle (`src/partition/partition-participant-transaction-pause.js:33–51`). Thus a follower can return OPENED, become leader through the specified transfer, and invalidate its admission while its old receipt remains in the set. The proposed confirmation checks command/lifecycle, but does not say that term/leader change invalidates those collected receipts or causes fresh projection confirmation. CUT_OVER/slot clear can therefore consume historical OPENED evidence while a required service is currently closed. Separately, target-specific RESUME commands have distinct applications; “every local receipt binds the same durable command” (:1812) needs an exact definition of authorization/manifest identity versus each target command/index.

**Required correction:** keep connection reopening/transfer before the final authorized resume/receipt collection, or explicitly define the revised local admission fence and the receipt invalidation/reconfirmation algorithm at that existing owner. Preserve deterministic replicated apply and current local actuation checks. Name the common authorization/manifest and each target-specific command receipt unambiguously. Required schedule changes leader/term after an early OPENED receipt and before final confirmation; it must remain pending or revalidate/reopen the affected target, then reach a complete current local result. Do not remove existing fencing merely to accept the historical receipt set.

### R5-4 — HIGH — the remote startup path still lacks its cold route and revision-proof producer/consumer handoff

The first-birth empty-registration split and proposed remote CDC adapter are useful explicit changes. D:1631–1639 and :1841–1850 nevertheless treat named existing components as providing a cold remote route and a complete partition/configuration/applied-revision answer without defining the required extensions at their actual boundaries.

Actual source facts:

- `BootstrapAPI.executeBootstrapControlPlaneQuery` delegates to the normal gateway/query engine with pressure and recovery-readiness options (`src/bootstrap/bootstrap-api-control-plane-methods.js:160–178`). The option is not itself a route producer.
- `BootstrapTopologySnapshotOwner` derives its published views from observed/retained rows; its local authoritative read still obtains partition IDs from the cache (`src/bootstrap/owners/bootstrap-topology-snapshot-owner-published-views.js:124–135`; `…-authoritative-rows.js:447–491`). An empty cold cache does not yield a remote service endpoint through that method.
- There is a real existing join handoff: `startup-sql-runtime-handoff.js:112–123` seeds an overlay from an already-received bootstrap response/snapshot. Its overlay consumes PARTITIONS and SERVICES rows (`src/query/sql-query-engine-bootstrap-routing-overlay.js:460–505`). This supports a join with such an envelope; it does not identify who obtains the envelope on an existing-origin seed restart before those projections exist. Recovery routing itself still needs routable services (`src/query/query-executor-canonical-leader-routing-methods.js:210–228`). Its priority grace also requires published metadata/readiness, rather than creating missing route facts (`src/query/query-executor-priority-recovery-bootstrap-routing.js:132–151`).
- The actual remote SQL read witness records partition, serving node/replica, term, role and observed time; it does **not** produce configuration key or applied revision (`src/partition/partition-service-entry-apply-base.js:560–579`). D:1848’s stronger same-revision completeness rule therefore needs an explicit producer/response contract, not an assumed existing field.
- The handoff after early recovery must also satisfy registration, not just later hydration. `SeedRegistrationPhase` waits for its required leadership cut (:107–125,153–156). `SeedPartitionsPhase` accepts local leadership or its existing bypasses; the canonical remote branch requires cache-resolved leader service (:581–599,651–672). The direct early bypass applies only without an SQL engine (:632–648), whereas this proposal creates that engine early. D does not say exactly when the validated early rows/routes are handed to this registration gate. The bootstrap writer can forward a write through a hosted follower (`src/cdc/cdc-bootstrap-direct-sql.js:121–143`); this is **not** a finding that all registration writes necessarily require a local leader. The missing contract is the gate/route/validated-row handoff before normal hydration, with no fabricated rows or readiness bypass.

**Required correction:** name the existing seed-contact/bootstrap owner and exact request/response that acquires cold route candidates for restart, distinguishes candidate hints from owner-proven current reads, and handles redirect/loss under the original deadline. Explicitly expand the actual read witness/snapshot producer to supply complete coherent configuration/applied-revision evidence and name its consumer. Bind the resulting validated route/projection to registration's existing prerequisite and the new remote handshake, then to normal hydration/verification. The document can propose these changes; they need not already be implemented. What is blocked is attributing those guarantees to current methods without their owner expansion. Required positive enters normal startup with no seeded cache, existing origins, remote control/system leaders and actual old CDC debt; the corresponding stale/partial answer must HOLD and later recover through the same owner.

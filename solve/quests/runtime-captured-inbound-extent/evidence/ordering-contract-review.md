## One proposed bounded-service and answer contract

The sole ordering owner remains `raft-rs-runtime-owner.js`; the operation port supplies lifecycle admission and canonical intent. No message priority classifier, second term/leadership authority, live drain, inspection loop or automatic reissue is added.

1. **Capture:** after execution/reconstruction admission succeeds, synchronously capture the current generation and the full finite inbound extent. Include input accepted while the command waited in the FIFO or while `ensureExecution` awaited. Exclude later appends from this command's predecessor extent. Capture is an ordering cut; it is not the operation's commit or application point.
2. **Prefix:** process every captured occurrence in order through existing ingress/local-log/native/Ready owners. A handled local/native refusal consumes its own occurrence, never substitutes for the selected command's answer, and does not discard the remaining captured tail (CA1/M9). A terminal hold/failure stops under its existing policy. Preserve persistence admission, lifecycle, identity and recovery policies.
3. **Actual state at operation entry:** after the prefix, revalidate the existing generation/health guard before dequeue/continuation and native entry. D1/D2 read native status and ConfState and step the transfer synchronously without an intervening await (`runtime-owner:1344-1369,1567-1586`). D3 invokes native propose and classifies its actual refusal synchronously (`:1589-1599,1655-1693`). A native term, membership or role transition already performed by the prefix must be honored. A merely queued late envelope is not such a transition.
4. **Answer:** the returned record describes that operation's actual invocation and existing Ready outcome. It does not assert that no later message exists, that the answering replica remains leader when the caller receives the response, or that the requested transfer/write completed. Never re-label an operation that may have had an effect as a harmless stale refusal and automatically retry it. Preserve existing unknown-outcome handling and exact write identity; no new retry permission follows from this ordering proposal.
5. **Remainder:** later accepted envelopes remain in the original group queue with the original wake/FIFO continuation. They must subsequently reach the native core or their existing typed terminal lifecycle/failure policy; a timer consumed while busy already transfers work into the group FIFO (`:1779-1851`). CA5 remains: no listener/status observer nests a new drain into announcement. A role observation remains an observation, not a new permit (`:1392-1455`).

This bounds the number of predecessor inbound occurrences by the captured N. It does **not** bound N, queued-command latency, individual persistence/send time, total memory, every recovery duration, or general distributed liveness. Frozen W07 measures actual selected native `propose` entry, not commitment/application (`runtime-captured-inbound-service-native.test.js:1-4`); no broader endpoint is inferred.

If approved, the exact superseded term is F1's extension from the admitted prefix to every envelope arriving during an in-turn await, plus its resulting cross-order answer/outbound/effect equivalence. Preserve equivalence and native controls for input in the captured prefix; preserve truthful actual-state decisions, CA1/M9, CA5, typed admission/failure, durability, exact replay, and recovery. Do not make the historical file green by silently editing its assertions. A separately identified contract/witness/impact registration must carry the decision together.

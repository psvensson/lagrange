DECISION

Select atomic RETIRE_AND_APPROVE over the split durable pending handoff.
The existing synchronous application owner can perform retirement, exact child
disposition, any accepted release transition, receipt replacement and applied
position in one SQLite transaction at one actual Raft position. No inspected
owner requires two log positions. This is a smaller source-compatible design
direction; it removes the pending-K redrive state, future native occurrence
escrow and the interval in which another retirement could overtake K.

This is ACCEPT for that architectural selection, not production approval.
The initial exact contract needs the corrections/qualifications N1-N7 below.
Its logical arithmetic and A-class exchange are conditionally coherent. Current
native finite custody, intake, reconstruction, physical memory, closure fairness,
snapshot import, complete settlement and deadline contracts remain HOLD. No
native RED/green or production defect is newly established by this source vet.

PRESERVED DRAFT 1 REFUTATIONS (NOT NATIVE FAILURES)

D1. A fixed cell carrying arbitrary K lacked a selected maintenance identity and
    losing-candidate result policy. Different RETIRE_FOR_APPROVE commands for G
    could compete; one cell cannot retain arbitrarily many current-G refusals.

D2. Its pre-core "routine RETIRE" gate was insufficient for already-owned stale
    retirement occurrences, an ambiguous second compound request, or a replay
    after another handoff existed. Deterministic pending-K/generation matching
    at apply and exact clear-CAS were required, not inferred from a local map.

D3. At handoff apply four ordinary A requests could already own Q_A=4. Promising
    a future K occurrence then exceeded that Q without independent escrow. The
    four-KiB receipt could not hold a full sixteen-KiB K; nested encoding also
    needed a whole-proposal limit. This was not harmless bookkeeping.

D4. Allowing competing ordinary APPROVE while handoff pending could change
    release state, fill other critical history and block required maintenance.
    Protecting K's result alone did not establish all closure/settlement progress.

D5. Continuous inbound arrival during drainReady awaits could keep drainInbound
    nonempty forever before the handoff's turn (S4). A reserved slot was not
    service. No current bounded scheduler supplied the asserted opportunity.

D6. New durable handoff discovery had to precede OPEN/build/census exits (S7).
    APPLIED_PROGRESS was not relayed to that continuation (S8). These were new
    obligations, not evidence that current service registration was broken.

D7. A locally timed-out operation or unknown native transfer could not discharge
    durable handoff debt. Four hanging recovery tasks could consume every current
    attempt slot (S7/S9). Discarding a native occurrence did not erase a committed
    handoff; this created a second recovery lifecycle that needed explicit proof.

D8. Trusted size/class fields, wrong parent/child identity, stale live inventory,
    counter exhaustion, follower backlog and application-image import were not
    solved by a pending cell. All would have needed additional owner contracts.

Atomic parent application removes D2's inter-entry window, D3's future native-K
occurrence, and D6/D7's orphan durable handoff. It does not remove native parent
custody, source transport limits, or snapshot/retention dependencies.

INITIAL ATOMIC CONTRACT FINDINGS — COMPLETE BATCH

N1. Tick service and lane-bound wording need precision (contract :186-201).
    "consensus inbound/tick" is one lane with no selection rule between its two
    kinds. If an always-nonempty inbound queue always wins, owed ticks starve.
    Select one owed tick AND one captured inbound envelope per round, or an
    explicitly alternating finite policy; then M/H/settlement/ordinary. The
    operation-count guarantee is conditional on each head operation returning.
    Other lanes' current custody is not proved bounded by this release profile.
    S4/S6/S7 supply the relevant current limits. Do not claim normal/TX quotas
    are implemented merely because a new runtime service rule is selected.

N2. Pre-core process loss is weaker than a durable handoff (contract :19-25,
    :126-133, :177-184). Minimal trace: reserve M locally -> process dies before
    native transfer or persistent bytes -> authoritative proof fences all
    transfers -> no surviving M bytes. The allowed discard endpoint terminates
    that opportunity; finite later stability cannot make that same lost M apply.
    If exact bytes survive, redrive them; otherwise report typed discard/unknown
    and keep any fresh recovery intent distinct. Limit exact-request eventual
    application to a surviving/re-driven opportunity. Never equate a local map
    miss with proof of this trace's authoritative discard. S4-S7 show the source
    boundaries; no new durable enqueue was authorized.

N3. Closure saturation still needs an owned progression (contract :99-110).
    Full HOLD history while OPEN cannot rely on the current continuation's OPEN
    early return (S7). A necessary pure RETIRE needs an explicit trigger and
    wake/re-read action. More strongly, the proposed rules permit the schedule:
    H result history full -> pure RETIRE frees H -> competing invalid/stale
    current-G HOLD requests consume H before desired closure gains custody ->
    repeat. A service lane does not reserve result admission for closure before
    that race. Either prove the selected producer/gate makes this schedule
    unreachable, select an equivalent atomic protected HOLD child, or retain an
    explicit nonstarvable-closure blocker. The A atomic child solves the A race;
    it must not be claimed as proof for a different H protocol. This is a
    contract-reachable trace, not a demonstrated native HOLD bug (HOLD absent).

N4. Parent receipt needs an exact selected schema (contract :57-69).
    Pin the format literal/variant for compound versus pure retirement. The
    childConflictWinnerOrNull field must retain KNOWN / UNAVAILABLE /
    NOT_APPLICABLE from the preceding identity contract, or make the receipt
    contain only an explicitly typed immutable child-result reference. Null
    must not erase an original distinction and later be filled with today's
    winner. Detailed result and retained receipt must agree on M/K/position.
    If lastM still promises child detail but it is unreadable/absent, report the
    inconsistency; do not treat the missing row as a fresh request.

N5. Typed failure edges are not yet complete (contract :75-88, :156-161).
    MAINTENANCE_BUSY is selected, but prose "typed profile/retention HOLD" and
    "protocol violation" are not exact caller outcomes. Select pre-core
    MALFORMED/identity mismatch/future generation/size/profile/counter results;
    distinguish deterministic old history from unavailable inventory and an
    impossible invalid committed command. Enumerate missing or inconsistent
    receipt/result, wrong partition/origin or childG, and moved origin. A
    well-formed unsupported retained profile must not be truncated to fit.
    State which stop re-drives, which waits on a named event, and which requires
    owner intervention. This is required by the design-note typed-edge section;
    it is not a request for a new error authority.

N6. State the internal atomic prefix rule (contract :11-15, :80).
    Parent validates current fromG at the real preceding application prefix.
    Child history validation sees fromG+1 inside the SAME transaction after the
    mechanical history transition. Its release-state/membership/query predicate
    facts are those of the real prefix; retirement changes none of those facts.
    Otherwise running the ordinary future-G gate against the old prefix would
    refuse every embedded child. There is no synthetic child log position.
    Specify whether conflict winner evidence is read before deletion or becomes
    tagged UNAVAILABLE after legitimate retirement. It cannot be substituted by
    the current winner. S1/S3 make this composition possible, not automatic.

N7. Selection is conditional, not a current finite-custody mechanism.
    Interfaces at :226-234 honestly are NEW, but their proof obligations remain
    substantial: all local/forwarded transfers before claim; exact occurrence
    association; current-term prefix and stale transfer fencing; live-ticket
    overlay during suffix scan; replacement delta; no release on timeout/ACK;
    bounded intake/whole-log restoration; and snapshot boundary. An old leader's
    delayed forwarded proposal can arrive after a successor's empty local map;
    a current-term barrier alone does not fence arbitrary pre-core late messages.
    The sender/runtime generation/transfer classification must establish that
    no extra creation escapes the same gate. S4-S6/S10 demonstrate why today's
    APIs do not provide this. Preserve this explicit implementation HOLD even
    after correcting N1-N6. No local pending Map is an acceptable substitute.

ACCOUNTING CHECK

Prior conditional projection:
  (128+4+8)*(24576+1024) + (32+2+4+1)*16384 + 20*1024
  = 4,243,456 bytes.
Additional independent M future-child entitlement 25,600 and future receipt
4,096 give 4,273,152 bytes. This arithmetic is correct. One parent native
occurrence is charged; its child is a logical subrequest, not another proposal.

Under conforming Q_A<=4, with every old-G retained A result eligible for
retirement, atomic deletion leaves <=4 ordinary pending unique entitlements.
Adding one child detailed result gives <=5, below C_A=8. Old-G pending copies
remain charged until actual apply/discard and cannot regain authority. Exact
physical duplicates consume occurrence Q but share semantic result entitlement.

This is NOT a production bound, physical-byte bound or follower bound. The
full encoded parent INCLUDING wrapper is limited to 16,384, so its child cannot
also independently consume that whole limit plus uncharged metadata. Maximum
encoding proof for result/key/receipt variants remains required. A child whose
full canonical bytes do not fit must be refused BEFORE custody, never truncated.
Old receipt storage plus reserved future receipt is correctly counted. A
snapshot/install must preserve both fixed receipt and detailed outcome at the
same boundary; SQLite intermediate pages/WAL/native/base64 copies are outside
this declared logical projection. Ineligible pinned rows invalidate the exchange
lemma; acknowledged TX/query obligations must survive in their existing owners.

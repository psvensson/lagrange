F3 — Do not substitute a historical low-level host driver for the live runtime,

F4 — No selected witness combines suffix replacement, duplicate local and

intended contract but cannot certify that composition or bytes.

F5 — Fair service under pressure is new work. enqueue's Promise tail serializes
turns but provides no bounded handoff service class; the selected tests have
finite controlled predecessors. A pending handoff queue flag is not a progress
proof. The future model must separately represent capacity admission and
scheduler service, pre-existing consensus occurrences, finite predecessor
work, repeated suffix discard, and closure/settlement competition.

The handcrafted snapshot transaction rollback test is not a legal application
snapshot carrying handoff, receipt and uncommitted custody. The future design
needs the snapshot boundary and recovery owner decision before implementation.

F7 — The selected transport schedules explicitly return/throw or are released
by the test. They do not establish bounded completion of a transport Promise
that never resolves, release outer-deadline propagation, late client response
handling, or messageRouter registration/delivery semantics. The current peer
delivery implementation awaits its transport Promise (peer-delivery:109-127).

No production defect is newly established by this read-only round. F1-F7 are

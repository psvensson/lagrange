Findings promoted into steering MUST be written as a normative sentence containing a recognized normative keyword (one of MUST, MUST NOT, SHALL, SHALL NOT, NEVER, DO NOT, FORBIDDEN, REQUIRED, SHOULD, MAY, or a sentence-leading ONLY — see findings/README.md for the authoritative list), because the steering pack generator infers rule strength from the body text and ignores front-matter strength and domain fields.
